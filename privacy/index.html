Privacy policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

In short: our apps do not collect, store or share any personal data. What you enter in an app stays on your device.

Who we are

This policy applies to all mobile and web apps published by Strawbr (on Google Play, the App Store and the web) and to this website. The data controller is:

Strawbr SAS
205 avenue des Bigochets, 78670 Villennes-sur-Seine, France
Email: contact@strawbr.com

Data we collect

None. Our apps do not require an account and do not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. They do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

Some apps save your preferences or choices (for example, items you mark as favorites) so that you find them the next time you open the app. This information is stored only on your device. We cannot access it, and it is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Permissions

If an app asks for a permission on your device, it is only to provide the feature you are using. Any data accessed through that permission stays on your device.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any personal data with third parties.

Google Play and the App Store may give us anonymous, aggregated statistics (such as the number of installs or crash reports), under their own privacy policies. This information does not allow us to identify you.

This website

This website does not use cookies or tracking tools. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may record technical data such as your IP address for security purposes, as described in the GitHub privacy statement.

Children

Our apps can be used by people of all ages. Since they collect no personal data, they collect no data from children.

Retention and deletion

We do not keep any personal data about you. To delete the information stored by an app on your device, clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and to data portability. To exercise these rights or ask any question, write to contact@strawbr.com. You can also file a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL (cnil.fr).

Security

Because we collect no personal data, none is stored on our servers. Data saved by our apps on your device is protected by your device's own security.

Changes to this policy

If an app starts collecting data, we will update this policy before that version is released. The date at the top of this page shows the latest update.

Contact

For any question about this policy, contact us at contact@strawbr.com.
